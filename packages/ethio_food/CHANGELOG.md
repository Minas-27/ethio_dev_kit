## 0.1.0

- First release: 722 foods and 39 nutrients from the Ethiopian Food Composition Table 2025 (EPHI and FAO).
- English and Amharic (Ge'ez) names, Amharic in Latin letters, and search words for every food.
- `EthioFood.search` in English or Amharic; Amharic letters that sound alike match each other.
- `Food.per(grams)` portions and `Meal` totals, with adult Daily Value percentages.
- Fasting status, everyday-food flag, food groups, and the source's quality flags on each value.
